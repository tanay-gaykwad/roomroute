# Code of Conduct

RoomRoute welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific in technical feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not on people.
- Share only information you have permission to disclose.
- Make discussions useful for the people trying to improve the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact option on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/tanay-gaykwad). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. The maintainer will review reports and may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or close a discussion. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
