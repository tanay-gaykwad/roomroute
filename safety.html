BEFORE YOU COMMIT

A few careful steps
make a difference.

Meet at the property, see the exact room, and review the written terms before transferring money.

  1. See the place in person.Confirm the room, shared spaces, building access, water, light, and surroundings match what you discussed.
  2. Confirm who can rent it.Ask for reasonable proof that the person can offer the property and get contact details for follow-up.
  3. Write down every cost.Record rent basis, deposit, maintenance, electricity, notice period, and refund terms before paying.
  4. Keep payment traceable.Avoid pressure, cash-only demands, and advance transfers before you have independently checked the property.

What “verified” means here

Email verification only confirms access to an email address. It does not prove identity, ownership, room condition, or a review's accuracy. Do not share passwords, OTPs, or sensitive identity documents in chat.
